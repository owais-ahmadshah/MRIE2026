CALL FOR PAPER

Multidisciplinary Research and Innovations in Engineering – 2026

MRIE-2026 invites submissions for its diverse range of tracks aimed at exploring cutting-edge research and innovations in various fields of technology and engineering. Researchers, practitioners, and academicians are encouraged to contribute their original work to foster interdisciplinary discussions and advancements in the following tracks:

  • Track 1: Advanced Computing Technologies
  • Track 2: Cybersecurity and Human Interaction
  • Track 3: Emerging Communication Networks & Security
  • Track 4: Power, Signals, Integrated Systems and Future Technologies
  • Track 5: Materials Science and Engineering

Papers from other topics relevant to the conference theme or the special sessions are also welcome.

Authors are encouraged to submit original, unpublished papers via the conference submission system. All submissions will undergo a thorough peer-review process. Accepted and presented papers will be considered for possible publication in IEEE Xplore.

Join us at MRIE 2026 to engage in insightful discussions, exchange ideas, and foster collaborations across diverse domains of engineering research and innovation.
